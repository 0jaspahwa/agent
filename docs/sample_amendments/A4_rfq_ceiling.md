# Corvo Engineering Pvt Ltd: Procurement Policy Amendment A-4 (RFQ ceiling)

Reference: CORVO-PROC-POL-04, amends version 5.0
Effective: 1 November 2026
Issued by: Head of Procurement

Large quotations have been closed without the wider competition of a proposal round. The ceiling between
quotations and proposals is lowered.

1. Clause 5.5 (RFQ). A requirement valued below Rs 7,50,000 (previously: below Rs 10,00,000) that is not
   placed under clauses 5.1 to 5.4 goes to RFQ with 3 suggested suppliers.

2. Clause 5.6 (RFP). A requirement valued at Rs 7,50,000 or more goes to RFP with 5 suggested suppliers.

All other clauses of version 5.0 remain unchanged.
