# Corvo Engineering Pvt Ltd: Procurement Policy Amendment A-3 (Price variance control)

**Reference:** CORVO-PROC-POL-04, amends version 5.0
**Effective:** 1 January 2027
**Issued by:** Head of Procurement

Recent spot purchases have repeated prices far above the item master standard price. A price check is added before the spot rule.

**New clause 5.3B, inserted after clause 5.3 and before clause 5.4.** *"Where a requirement is valued from purchase history under clause 3.1(b), and its valuation unit price is more than 15% above the item master standard price, it must not be placed as a spot PO. It goes to RFQ with 3 suggested suppliers if its value is below ₹10,00,000; otherwise clause 5.6 (RFP) applies. Contract POs (5.2) and emergency POs (5.3) are not affected."*

The price premium is measured as (valuation unit price − standard price) ÷ standard price.

All other clauses of version 5.0 remain unchanged.
