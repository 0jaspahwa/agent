# Corvo Engineering Pvt Ltd: Procurement Policy Amendment A-1

**Reference:** CORVO-PROC-POL-04, amends version 5.0
**Effective:** 1 December 2026
**Issued by:** Head of Procurement

Following the half-year audit, the direct-purchase limits are changed as follows.

1. **Clause 5.4 (Spot PO).** The spot purchase limit is reduced. A requirement valued **at or below ₹1,50,000** (previously: below ₹2,00,000) may be placed as a spot PO with the 1st preferred supplier, provided the item was bought from an Active supplier in the last 365 days.

2. **Clause 5.3 (Emergency PO).** The emergency purchase limit is raised from ₹5,00,000 to **₹7,50,000**. Emergency requirements valued at or below ₹7,50,000 may be placed as an emergency PO under the existing conditions.

All other clauses of version 5.0 remain unchanged.
