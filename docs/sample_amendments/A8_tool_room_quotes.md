# Corvo Engineering Pvt Ltd: Procurement Policy Amendment A-8 (Tool Room purchases)

**Reference:** CORVO-PROC-POL-04, amends version 5.0
**Effective:** 1 November 2026

The Tool Room's spot purchases are to be quoted for above a modest value.

**New clause 5.3D, inserted after clause 5.3 and before clause 5.4.** *"A requirement that includes at least
one PR line raised by the Tool Room department, and whose value is ₹1,00,000 or more, must not be placed as
a spot PO. It goes to RFQ with 3 suggested suppliers if its value is below ₹10,00,000; otherwise clause 5.6
(RFP) applies. Contract POs (5.2) and emergency POs (5.3) are not affected."*

A requirement counts as a Tool Room requirement if any one of its clubbed lines comes from the Tool Room,
even when the other lines come from other departments.

All other clauses of version 5.0 remain unchanged.
