# Corvo Engineering Pvt Ltd: Procurement Policy Amendment A-2 (Chakan plant controls)

**Reference:** CORVO-PROC-POL-04, amends version 5.0
**Effective:** 1 December 2026
**Applies to:** plant CHK1 (Chakan) only. Plant PUN1 (Bhosari) is not affected.

Chakan's lubricant consumption has risen sharply, and several spot orders were placed without competitive quotes. Two controls are added for Chakan.

1. **Spot PO limit at Chakan.** For requirements at CHK1, the spot PO limit in clause 5.4 is **below ₹1,00,000** instead of below ₹2,00,000. The other conditions of clause 5.4 are unchanged. PUN1 keeps the ₹2,00,000 limit.

2. **New clause 5.3A: Lubricants at Chakan go to quotes.** Insert after clause 5.3 and before clause 5.4: *"A CHK1 requirement for an item in the category Lubricants that is not placed on a rate contract (5.2) or as an emergency PO (5.3) must go to RFQ with 3 suggested suppliers, whatever its value, unless its value is ₹10,00,000 or more, in which case clause 5.6 (RFP) applies."*

All other clauses of version 5.0 remain unchanged.
