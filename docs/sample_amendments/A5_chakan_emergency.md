# Corvo Engineering Pvt Ltd: Procurement Policy Amendment A-5 (Chakan emergency limit)

**Reference:** CORVO-PROC-POL-04, amends version 5.0
**Effective:** 1 November 2026
**Applies to:** plant CHK1 (Chakan) only.

Emergency purchases at Chakan are to be approved by the plant head above a lower threshold.

**Clause 5.3 (Emergency PO), Chakan only.** For requirements at CHK1, the emergency purchase limit is
**at or below ₹2,00,000** instead of at or below ₹5,00,000. The other conditions of clause 5.3 are unchanged.
An emergency requirement at CHK1 above ₹2,00,000 is decided by the following clauses (5.4 to 5.6) as usual.

Plant PUN1 keeps the ₹5,00,000 limit. All other clauses of version 5.0 remain unchanged.
