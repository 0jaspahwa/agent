# Corvo Engineering Pvt Ltd: Procurement Policy Amendment A-6 (Clubbing window)

**Reference:** CORVO-PROC-POL-04, amends version 5.0
**Effective:** 1 November 2026

Clubbing lines raised two weeks apart has delayed the earlier requester's material. The clubbing window
is shortened.

**Clause 2.2 (Clubbing).** Lines for the same item at the same plant are clubbed into one requirement when
they were created **within 7 calendar days** of the first line (previously: within 14 calendar days) and
their need-by dates are within 7 days of the first line's need-by date (unchanged).

All other clauses of version 5.0 remain unchanged.
