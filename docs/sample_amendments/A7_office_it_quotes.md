# Corvo Engineering Pvt Ltd: Procurement Policy Amendment A-7 (Office and IT purchases)

Reference: CORVO-PROC-POL-04, amends version 5.0
Effective: 1 November 2026
Issued by: Head of Procurement and Head of IT

IT hardware bought on spot orders has not met the standard configuration. Office and IT items are to be
quoted for.

New clause 5.3C, inserted after clause 5.3 and before clause 5.4:
"A requirement for an item in the category Office & IT that is not placed on a rate contract (5.2) or as
an emergency PO (5.3) must not be placed as a spot PO. It goes to RFQ with 3 suggested suppliers if its
value is below Rs 10,00,000; otherwise clause 5.6 (RFP) applies."

All other clauses of version 5.0 remain unchanged.
